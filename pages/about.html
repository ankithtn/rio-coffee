Who We Are

Backed by experience,
built for service.

Lohith Enterprises was established in 2018 as a dedicated vending machine service provider in Bangalore. The business operates in the coffee and tea beverages industry, serving as both a service provider and supplier for corporate beverage needs.

Rio Coffee is backed by Skanda Century Food Pvt Ltd, founded in 2006, bringing long-standing experience in coffee growing, curing, roasting, and vending solutions. Today, Rio serves MNCs and corporate companies across Bengaluru and Mysore.
